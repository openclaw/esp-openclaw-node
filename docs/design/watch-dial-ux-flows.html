SIZING & CALIBRATION REVIEW / OUTSIDE THE DEVICE UX

Is a target really nine millimetres?

UNCALIBRATED

This layer reviews physical size only; it is not part of the device interface and nothing in it sends an action. Your browser cannot tell this page how large a millimetre is on your monitor: CSS millimetres are not physical millimetres, and device pixel ratio, retina scaling and screenshot resolution prove nothing about real-world size. So calibrate by hand with a physical ruler or an ID-1 bank card, at your current browser zoom, and recalibrate whenever you change zoom, monitor or window scaling.

STEP 4 / WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT PROVE

Limitations.

  • Geometry is a nominal calculation, not a measured active area. A 5-inch (127.00 mm) diagonal at 16:9 gives 110.69 × 62.26 mm and 1280 / 110.69 = 11.564 px/mm (≈ 293.7 ppi). M5Stack publishes the enclosure as 128 × 80 × 12 mm — that is the case, not the glass. Bezel, panel variant and vendor tolerance can move these numbers.
  • Nothing here is verified on a device. No hardware was used. Physical device and firmware proof is tracked separately and is blocked; this page is a browser simulation with synthetic data.
  • Landscape is a design choice. This revision targets 1280 × 720 landscape. Auto-rotation is not claimed or verified.
  • No input assumptions beyond a finger. The panel is documented as capacitive touch (2026 variants ST7121/ST7123, earlier units GT911). Hover, pen precision, multitouch gestures, pressure and physical keys are not assumed, and controller variants may differ in sampling and minimum contact area.
  • A simulator cannot prove legibility. Type sizes here are chosen against the nominal density; reading text at ~293.7 ppi still needs a hardware review before anything is called usable.
  • Calibration is per browser state and is not stored. It lives in memory for this page view only. Changing zoom, display or window scaling invalidates it; the badge returns to UNCALIBRATED when a device pixel ratio change is detected, but it cannot catch every case.
